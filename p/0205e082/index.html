MiXa Site-Builder App

Enabling users create stunning websites, blogs, and documentation and effortless content publishing with zero coding skills

Problem space

Mixa is an innovative platform that empowers users to create stunning websites, blogs, and documentation sites with zero coding skills. The platform is designed for versatility, accommodating notes and blogs. As a Mixa team, we started a new project from the ground up to meet the growing demand for easy-to-use website creation and strong SEO. Our innovative app challenges platforms like Wix and WordPress by simplify website creation

Project discovery

Collecting Proof – Understanding User Drive

We conducted 15 individual interviews and gathered responses from 20 online surveys to acquire both quantitative and qualitative data.

Some Insights

This research illuminated a specific issue: complexity of current website builder apps.

Our participants, aged 20 to 45, offered insights into user preferences, habits, and expectations related to website builder applications.

They want a platform more geared towards creatives and that was cleaner and easier to use than their current options.

Personas

We wanted to form a deeper understanding of our users' goals, needs, experiences, and behaviours. So, we created 4 personas for each of our user segments.

User Journey

I collaborated with my product partner to create an journey map for our user's scheduling task. This allowed us to establish a mutual understanding of the user's motivations from the outset, enabling us to progress together.

Summary of the Journey Map

  1. Learn about main features they need: Selected essential paths aligning with user goals: Markdown publishing, live-preview editor, Templates, Pricing, and SEO.

  2. Learned about their content and Marketing Strategy: Challenges in customizing Templates and understanding Pricing plans; prioritized clarity and simplicity.

  3. What changed in the design due to user journey mapping? Enhanced customization options, clarified explanations, and visual aids for Pricing plans to improve user experience.

Wireframes - Exploration & feasibility

  • I teamed up with our talented developers to brainstorm a better feature.

  • This cool tool lets users publish their blogs across all platforms at once, boosting their results without any hassle.

  • I sketched out wireframes and gathered feedback from my triad.

Usability Testing

I conducted 4 usability tests focused on understanding user thought processes rather than task completion. Identified recurring issues through rainbow spreadsheet analysis and adjusted UI elements to enhance user experience.

issue

During testing, a common problem emerged with confusing navigation in the live-preview editor, affecting 3 out of 7 tests. Users found the interface counterintuitive and struggled to locate editing tools.

solution

We redesigned the navigation layout, emphasizing clarity and intuitive placement of editing tools through three design iterations. Follow-up testing with 5 participants confirmed the effectiveness, with 4 out of 5 successfully completing tasks without difficulty, validating the solution."

Rapid Prototypes

After gathering feedback from the wireframe stage, I promptly transitioned to creating a prototype within the allotted time frame. Leveraging insights into the user's journey and motivations, I developed an immersive UX design that emphasized user needs and key actions.

All participants found the features and user experience intuitive and simple to navigate, resulting in an exceptional ease-of-use score of 5.44 out of 5.

Retrospective

All test participants found the feature easy-to-use. Some users expressed the need to better understand how the technology worked, to generate the best times. The final design reflects our deep understanding of users' preferences and behaviours gathered through extensive research and usability testing.

I realized there is opportunities to improve side bar by using A/B testing

Learnings

  • This project significantly contributed to my growth as a designer by enhancing my problem-solving skills, user-centered design approach, and ability to adapt to emerging technologies.

  • Accessibility considerations were not included due to time constraints. They definitely need to be added at a future point in time.
