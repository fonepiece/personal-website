Flair Airlines Booking Re- Design

Design Sprint Project to Improve Urgent Problem

Design Sprint Project

The Flair Airlines Booking Platform Redesign project aims to modernize and optimize the booking experience for Flair Airlines' customers. By enhancing usability, functionality, and design, the project seeks to boost user satisfaction and drive increased bookings.

Problem

The website date booking system have issues. Customer may get confused during the process of selecting dates. The function combines the process of selecting departure and return dates in just one go.

Solution

Simplify the booking process by reducing the number of steps and providing clear guidance at each stage. Implement a progress indicator to help users understand where they are in the process.

Goal

The primary goal is to enhance the usability of the booking platform. This entails streamlining the booking process, reducing user friction, and making it easier for customers to search for and book flights

Day 1: Understand the challenge and build the map

Each member spent a good amount of time looking into what is working and what is not working on the website. And had some thoughts written down on what can be improved.

Biggest Challenges to Reach the Goals

Some thoughts written down on what can be solution for improvements.

Combine our ideas into one single flow!

After gathering all the ideas and mapping them, it became more clear about what is a constant issue in the website. It was the Flight Date function that many have encounter some confusion and set back. There was plenty of room for improvement on the function and interface.

Day 2: Sketch Solutions

Sketching solutions in UX design is vital, enabling rapid idea visualization, collaboration, user-centered design, and concept validation. It's cost-efficient, minimizes risks, and fosters effective communication, ensuring design excellence.

Lighting Demos

Here we take a look at other airlines to see how they manage the booking selection.

  • The use of color and/or separation of text boxes to differentiate departure and return dates,

  • Special deals integrated on dates to let user consider and for added user experience.

  • Minimum input from user to be able to see available flights and start choosing the flights.

Sketching: Notes, Doodles, Crazy 8’s & Solution Sketch

SketchHere, the team made sketches of how we can replicate and apply inspiration from how other companies are doing it. Each member are then allowed to have 2x votes for better and clear direction about what can be included in the final solution.

After the vote, we obtained the most creative and functional idea to solve our challenge, which is to improve the experience of selecting travel dates when searching for a flight on the Flair Airline page.

It was more clear how the solution can ideally include certain functions, layout, and feature. Although some parts may still not make it to the final cut (i.e. the total days). But, a different approach was applied to give similar visualization of how many days the trip will cover.

Guerrilla Usability Test

Test Plan Document for Guerrilla Usability Testing: Flair Airlines Ticket Selection

Official booking website experience

3 of 4 people experienced a bug on a flair flight booking website that disrupts the arrival date selection process, it generates frustration and annoyance…

There was a 75% dissatisfaction rate on date selection while booking a flight on flair airlines

Users are required to repeat the process, entering the same information again. However, some testers pointed out that this issue could be infuriating if it hadn't been a test. In real situations, flight opportunities get occupied within minutes, and this failure could cause users to lose the chance of a flight they had meticulously chosen. They might already have other contracted services, such as confirmed appointments, hotels, car rentals, etc.

What user said about our proposal design

We observed and had the answer by asking open ended questions. It shows that users prefer the design we created and they had pleasing user experience. During picking time there were no confusion and the completed task easily.

  • Clear and intuitive labels, buttons, and icons to guide users through the process, reducing confusion and friction.

  • Highlighting key information and make it easier for users to scan and compare options.

  • Clear Calls-to-Action

Challanges

It was difficult to choose what problem issue to solve as the team saw quite a few flaws. e.g. inconsistent topography, user flow and error handling.

Limited user testing - due to the time constraints, the number of participants involved was limited.

Takeaways

Confidence in Decision-Making - the process encouraged us to share any idea and decide. This increased the collaboration and belonging in the group
