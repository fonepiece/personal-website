Website UI/UX Redesign Project

Improved usability and navigation for the Sisters in Psychedelics's website's summit event attendees

Project Overview

This UX case study aimed to improve usability and navigation for the Sisters in Psychedelics's website's summit event attendees. The project focused on creating an immersive interface in line with the Psychedelics brand, emphasizing readability and urgency to drive ticket sales. Additionally, the website connects and supports women in psychedelic medicine, research, and therapy through community, resources, and engaging events. Through intuitive design and persuasive calls-to-action, the goal was to provide a seamless and engaging user experience, encouraging immediate and confident actions.

Project Goal

  • Improve usability and navigation for seamless user experience

  • Create a visually appealing and immersive user interface that aligns with the branding of Psychedelics

  • Optimize the readability and evoke urgency and encourage attendees to buy tickets for a summit event

Product Users

  • Diverse range of individuals who can benefit from and contribute to the safe and inclusive psychedelic space for women and underrepresented voices

Quantitative Research

Surveys played a crucial role in gathering essential user data and insights. Through meticulous planning, conducting, and thorough analysis of responses, I observed distinct patterns and made valuable inferences about user preferences. These insights were instrumental in informing design decisions, allowing me to enhance the overall user experience effectively. By leveraging the survey data, I tailored the interface and interactions to align closely with user needs and expectations, ensuring a more user-friendly and engaging platform.

Competitor Analysis

After the project kickoff, we outlined our research strategy and objectives. We analyzed competitors specified by the website owner, focusing on Coralus and The Forum. Coralus inspired our vibrant user experience with its focus on community connections, aligning with our goals. The Forum's emphasis on meaningful interactions made it a relevant competitor, essential for fostering engaging conversations on our platform promoting psychedelics and personal growth.

The website is to create a vibrant and inclusive space for individuals interested in psychedelics, personal growth, and community engagement.

Features:

  • Coralus' immersive design and captivating visuals set it apart, offering a unique and visually stimulating user experience.

  • The platform's focus on connecting individuals and fostering collaboration to build a supportive community.

The website aims to foster an inclusive and engaging community where individuals can participate in meaningful discussions, share knowledge, and build connections in a supportive environment.

Features:

  • The Forum's emphasis on fostering discussions and knowledge-sharing through forums aligns for community engagement.

  • The clean and straightforward design promotes easy access to information and encourages user participation.

Qualitative Research

To gather in-depth insights and rich, detailed responses from participants, we conducted the interviews with 30 website and psychedelic users.

Questions

  • How would you describe your overall experience with the current website design?

  • Are there any particular concerns about the credibility of the information presented on the website?

  • Are there any specific challenges you encounter when trying to access event details and buying tickets?

Learnings

I mastered Figma and InVision for interactive design, enhancing my technical abilities. Additionally, I improved my communication skills, focusing on empathetic understanding.
